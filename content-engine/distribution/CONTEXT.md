# Distribution — Smoke Content Engine

## What happens here
Finished masters go out. Platform crop, caption, schedule, light analytics.
Per-drop posts live in `../calendar/drops/NNN_slug/posts/`; the folders here
(`platforms/ scheduling/ analytics/`) hold cross-drop templates and rollups.

## Platforms
- X (primary): @smokering25 and founder handles
- Site embed: LilBlunt.win
- Secondary: TikTok / shorts if founder greenlights the same master

## Cadence (launch week)
- T-0: 30s master + 10s hero
- T+hours: hero 10s as quote-bait
- T+1d: behind-one-shot stills if renders exist
- Do not spam the same 30s more than twice without a new hook

## Steady-state cadence
Follow `../calendar/calendar.md` — weekly anchor (hype-30s), mid-week hero
(hero-10s), reactive filler (still-post / x-thread).

## Adaptation rules
- X: first line = hook; $SMOKE + Solana in body; link LilBlunt.win; ≤2 hashtags
- Site: no platform watermarks
- Never invent APY, MC, or "guaranteed SOL". Eligible for SOL rewards at bong
  party = the locked claim.

## Good work
- Caption matches the locked script promise
- 10s hero posted as its own asset, not only trimmed in-app
- UTM / short link noted if founder provides one
- After posting, capture numbers in `analytics/` and mark the drop `analyzed`

## Files
- `platforms/` — per-platform caption templates
- `scheduling/` — the posting calendar / queue
- `analytics/` — per-drop results + rollups (feeds calendar grooming)
