## Why they leave the hideout

Bull already sold the job. Bears hold the mine. The Winchester and the helmet cost one Bitcoin. Fort Knox is the stake. Blaze is why Bull is in the smelter. Gold Mine is why they ride. Diamonds stay in Episode 1.

They are not safe when they toast. The bears can follow a trail. That fact pays off on the way back.

## The run, in order

1. **Leave the hideout.** Target practice is over. Winchester is the founder rifle. They walk out together. Bull drives. Lil Blunt rides.
2. **The quad.** Claim run. Lil Blunt shoots bears from the back seat. Three hearts max, already locked for the descent, do not add a second health system.
3. **Spy point.** They stop where the vault road and a neighboring building are both visible. They do not ride in.
4. **Decoy.** Lil Blunt shoots the quad into that building. It explodes. Bears go to the fire. The quad is spent. It does not enter Fort Knox.
5. **The gold.** On foot, first person, one load. Fort Knox is the stake, not a loot closet. They take what they can carry.
6. **Inferno sacrificed.** Bull stays so Lil Blunt can get the load out. He is not dead on screen. He is taken or pinned. The player must believe he is lost.
7. **Trail back.** Lil Blunt returns alone with the gold and a line of bears behind him. The hideout is not safe. The decoy did not kill the trail.
8. **AwesomeX.** A dragon. He has machine guns. He hits the bear line and opens a path. He does not replace Bull and he does not take the stake.
9. **Rescue.** Lil Blunt and AwesomeX go back for Bull. Bull is pulled out. The gold is still theirs. AwesomeX is the burn that cleared the door, not the owner of the vault.

Auction waits. Do not open it on this run.
