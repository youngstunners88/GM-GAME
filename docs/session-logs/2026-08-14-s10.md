## Also fixed (non-threaded trap)
The committed `export_presets.cfg` and `scripts/export-web.sh` carried the stale
Godot-3.x `web/use_threads=true` keys. Godot 4.3 ignores that key (so builds were
non-threaded in practice — proven: the export never regenerated
`index.worker.js`), but it's the exact "local ≠ CI" trap the manifest warns
about. Both are now pinned to `variant/thread_support=false`, byte-matching the
CI preset, and the stale committed `web/game/index.worker.js` artifact was
removed.
