## B. Blaze Rush — visual beat sheet

**Background (depth, not noise)**
- Far: dark gradient void (near-black → deep teal/charcoal), slow parallax shapes at **10–15%** opacity (crystal shards / faint rings). No high-contrast edges.
- Mid: soft silhouettes only; scroll slower than playfield. Keep a **clear 40–50% center band** visually quieter so hazards pop.

**Colour language (readability first)**
- **Platforms:** mid warm grey or muted stone + thin bright top lip (cream/light teal). Always lighter than void, darker than collectibles.
- **Hazards:** hot danger — **orange-red / magma** with a 1–2px bright edge; never teal or gold. Spikes/pits read as “down = death” via strong value contrast, not detail.
- **Collectibles:** high-luma **gold or white-teal spark**; small, round, blink gently. Must out-bright platforms and never share hazard hues.
- Rule: if it kills you → warm/red; if you land on it → neutral stone; if you grab it → bright cool/gold. No camouflage.

**Protocol landmarks (3 beats, not spam)**
1. **Early (~10–15%):** **SmokeRing** — huge faded green rocket-ring mural in far BG; one pass, establishes FOMO pace.
2. **Mid (~45–55%):** **DIAMONDS** — teal gem formation as a gate/arch the run goes through; readable silhouette, still BG/mid, not a hitbox disguise.
3. **Late (~75–85%):** **GoldMine** — gold pickaxe crest on a wide safe platform roof or cliff face before the hard spike stretch; “last brand beat → final push.”

**One “wow” moment**
- Mid-run **crystal flare corridor**: brief tunnel where BG blooms teal-white, platforms get a thin diamond sheen, and a single large DIAMONDS gem landmark pulses once as speed feels to surge — then snaps back to sober contrast before the next hazard cluster.
