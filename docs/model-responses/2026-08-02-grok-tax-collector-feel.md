**1. Pacing risk**  
Continuous live chase + ranged while moving deletes the old safe windows (patrol downtime, stale charge dodge). Biggest unfairness risk: **no reliable reset space**. A first-timer gets pinned by movement pressure *and* clipboard arcs with nowhere to re-orient, heal, or learn. The fight stops reading as “dodge then punish” and becomes constant evasion.

**2. Minimum telegraph**  
Keep the project pattern. Before aggressive chase engages (from idle/patrol or after VULNERABLE): **~0.5s freeze, face player, clear wind-up pose/VFX** (same family as the regular Tax Collector’s 0.5s face-then-pursue). No instant lock-on sprint. After a clipboard throw while moving, a brief glide or hitch before re-accelerating also helps readability. VULNERABLE’s red flash stays the punish cue—don’t blur into it from full chase.

**3. One number**  
**Chase speed.** First pass: **~0.7–0.8× player move speed** (boss closes only if you hesitate or take a bad line; good movement still creates gap for jumps/throws to be reacted to). Faster than that and Stage-1 players get run down; jump CD and clipboard cadence matter less if they can’t outwalk the threat.

Threaten by denying *idle* safety, not by matching or beating pure escape speed.
