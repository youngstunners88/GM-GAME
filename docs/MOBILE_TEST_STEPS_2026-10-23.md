# Phone test steps - 2026-10-23

For Rich. Plain steps, no jargon. Please test on your phone (and the tablet if you can). Nothing here is "done" until you have tried it on a real phone.

## Before you start (every time)

1. Open the game and do a **hard refresh** (close the tab and open it again, or clear the page cache) so you get the newest version.
2. Start Stage 1. Look at the **BUILD** line in the **bottom-left corner**. It must be a **different** code from the last time you tested.
   If it is the same, you are still on the old version: refresh again before you test anything.
3. Hold the phone sideways (landscape) unless a step says otherwise.
4. There should be **no words and no key names** on any button anywhere. If you see "SPACE", "[E]", "press R", "TAP OUT (Q)" or any instruction text on a button, tell me where.
   (Words like BLAZE DIAMONDS, ATTEMPT, and the story text are meant to be there.)

## D. The jump from Episode 1 to Episode 2 (the part you care most about)

1. Beat the Stage 3 boss. Leave your thumb on the left side of the screen the whole time if you can.
2. You should see:
   - the boss music **fades out** (no old song coming back when the film ends),
   - the film plays (hold the skip button if you want to skip it),
   - the last picture of the film (Lil Blunt in the cart) **stays on screen**. After a moment a thin bar may appear at the bottom,
   - then it **dissolves** into the live tunnel. **No black screen, no blue screen, no words.**
   - the cart waits until the picture has dissolved, then the runner music fades in.
3. After it dissolves, nothing should be stuck: slide your thumb and the cart answers.
4. Also try: **main menu -> Episode 2 button**. You should see a calm dark picture with a small mine cart and a thin bar (no words), then the same dissolve.
5. Turn the phone while it is loading: the picture should stay centred.

**Please tell me:** how long the last picture of the film stayed before the tunnel appeared, whether the sound crackled or jumped at any point, whether anything froze, and whether the screen ever went black or blue.
