# YOUR ROLE: Grok 4.5 — protocol fantasy / identity

Two deliverables, tight and usable as in-game copy:

## 1. Diamond Vault clerk character
Invent a vault clerk/attendant NPC for the Diamond Vault that Lil Blunt talks
to (chill, positive, DIAMONDS-protocol flavored — three payout pools, ETH
rewards). Provide:
- Name + one-line visual description (must be buildable from simple Godot
  Polygon2D/Sprite2D primitives — no new art dependency).
- 4-6 short dialogue lines total covering: greeting, "how many $DIAMONDS to
  store/stake?", "how many Blaze Diamonds to crush?", confirm, and a
  nothing-to-stake line. Keep each line <= 90 chars. Positive/chill tone; no
  aggressive or stereotypical drug imagery.

## 2. Fort Knox identity (so it does NOT read as a Diamond Vault reskin)
Give a distinct GOLD MINE identity for Fort Knox's second chamber: theme, one
set-piece idea, and 2-3 lines of signage/flavor text. Wild-West gold-rush
DeFi framing (100-day miners, GOLD Rush Auctions, Fort Knox staking).

Copy only — no code. Keep it short.
