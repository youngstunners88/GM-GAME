# Creative brief — expand antagonist boss taunt vocabulary (Lil Blunt: The Smoke Realm)

You are a game writer for a chill, positive, retro-16-bit platformer. The hero
is **Lil Blunt**, a friendly weed-nugget mascot for three crypto protocols
(SmokeRing/SMOKE, DIAMONDS/ETH rewards, GoldMine/GOLD). The THREE BOSSES are the
antagonists — the founder says their voices "went silent" and their lines
"don't vary enough." We are adding more lines per category so they stop
repeating. Lines are short spoken taunts (1 short sentence, punchy, <= ~8 words,
readable in ~2s). They must be villainous but PG / cartoonish — no slurs, no
real drugs framed negatively, no gore. Crypto/DeFi wordplay is the whole point.

## The three antagonists & their voice
- **tax** — "IRS Auditor" / Tax Collector (Stage 1). Nasal, bureaucratic,
  condescending. Themes: taxes, audits, paperwork, seizing gains, FUD.
- **crystal** — "Crystal Distributor" (Stage 2). Cold, robotic, corporate.
  Themes: liquidation, buybacks, redistribution, ETH drainage, "efficiency."
- **bandit** — "Bandit" / Claim Jumper (Stage 3). Gravelly, wild-west, unhinged.
  Themes: dynamite, claim-jumping, minecarts, gold, "greenhorn."

## Categories (need MORE lines in each; give me NEW ones, not repeats of these)
- taunt (ambient, said repeatedly during fight) — want ~8 per boss
- mock (player just took damage) — want ~5 per boss
- hurt (boss just took damage) — want ~3 per boss
- phase50 / phase25 (one dramatic line each at 50%/25% HP) — want ~2 each
- death (boss defeated) — want ~2 each

## Existing lines (DO NOT duplicate; match the tone, then go beyond)
@include assets/boss-voices.json

## Output
For EACH boss give me clearly-labeled NEW lines per category (taunt/mock/hurt/
phase50/phase25/death). Rank strongest first. Keep them tight and on-brand.
Bonus: flag any of MY existing lines you think are weak or off-brand.
