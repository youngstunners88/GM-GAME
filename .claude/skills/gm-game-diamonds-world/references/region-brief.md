## Asset family
Prioritize faceted rock modules; three distinct CUT/WEIGH/STAMP desks; press housing; short stone bridge with visible banks; cyan lamp. Reuse modules with rotation/variants. Keep at most one dominant hero per camera view.

For each proposed prop, record: purpose, supporting surface, dimensions in world pixels and optional model metres, semantic front, top-down footprint, collision/decorative status, attachment face, source references, prompt, target atlas/triangles, pipeline/model, estimated cost and acceptance evidence. Require readable silhouette and a grounded base; reject fused objects, arbitrary text, cropped supports and mismatched camera perspective.

Prompt starter: “Isolated {prop}, crystalline pressure works with traversable terraces and an assay court, stylized hand-painted 2.5D game asset, fixed orthographic three-quarter view matching the production camera, blue slate #30495D, stone #597583, ice #B7DFE5; cyan #68D5E8 and sparse lime #7EE57C, complete grounded base, clear separated silhouette, no letters, logos, people or surrounding scene.” Refine against the actual camera before generation. A texture prompt instead describes a tileable ground material, flat lighting and no baked perspective/landmarks.

## Reject on review
Reject identical examiner silhouettes; scenery extending beyond camera bounds; decorative bridges over invisible walkable voids; blue full-screen glare. Also reject landmarks that promise interactions they do not have, hidden stop labels, loss of official study access, or a scene that reads as the other two regions with a tint.
