Read `00_VISUAL_BIBLE.md` first, section 5 MODE C. These are photoreal cinematic film stills with the mascot composited as a stylised 3D character, lit by the same light as the real people. Ages: all adults. Real clothing but no logos or lettering anywhere. Cannabis is shown as relaxed, communal and positive. 1:1, centred, circular safe zone, nothing in the corners.

Reference set for every community piece (2-4 each): `lb_ref_02_smoke_rocket_cowboy.png` (his face and cowboy costume), `STILL_lil-blunt_apose_v2.png` (his 3D skin and leather materials), `IMG_2480_action_jump.jpg` (how he is lit inside a fully photoreal scene). Per-piece extras are listed. Optional extra: `lbframe_lounge_diamond_28s.jpg` / `lbframe_lounge_crowd_12s.jpg` (real people in the $SMOKE LOUNGE, neon and sofa look).
