# Lil Blunt NFT Collection - founder brief (condensed from Google doc "NFTS", 2026-10-19)
Source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AzDsDVvyl8LA0hX6HABz3KIqzTW2xrBhA_z6Ek3BBjY (Drive id 1AzDsDVvyl8LA0hX6HABz3KIqzTW2xrBhA_z6Ek3BBjY).
Purpose: first visual set of the earned/community-facing Lil Blunt Collection, to show the Captain. Separate from the 888 Season Game Passes; the six soulbound achievement cards stay the tight proof-of-play subset.
Layers (target ~50/50, 12-16 pieces): GAME IMPLICIT (moments only a player lived) and COMMUNITY / CULTURAL REFLECTION (real people, male and female, in the Smoke Lounge / Bong Party world with Lil Blunt as an equal).
Pillars: Lil Blunt always recognisable and never aggressive; the Smoke Lounge is the cultural living room (low light, neon, deep greens and golds, velvet, glass, hanging plants, haze); game moments stay true to Smoke Realm / Crystal Caverns / Gold Rush palettes; community pieces feel real (diverse bodies, ages, skin tones, not fashion-model stiff); poetic but grounded, like a still from a short film; 1:1 first, 4:5 for cinematic ones.
First wave - Game: 1 Smoke Realm double jump; 2 over the defeated Tax Collector/Auditor in the GOV VAULT with the pickaxe; 3 Blaze Mode through Crystal Caverns; 4 crystal titan smash opens the gold path; 5 skull minecart after Claim Jumper, Bitcoin tokens in the air; 6 alone in the Smoke Lounge looking at a floating ETH ring / Diamond Shard; 7 protocol portal ladder glow, whitepaper/scorecard room; 8 secret alcove find.
First wave - Community: 1 mixed group round the table, Lil Blunt at the centre; 2 woman on the Lounge couch with Lil Blunt, mutual respect; 3 man in workwear and Lil Blunt over a glowing Gold Mine / Fort Knox map; 4 community circle, people of colour, different body types; 5 people + Lil Blunt near Logan's Lake / the day-88 river; 6 high-status chill group with subtle $SMOKE/$DIAMONDS/$GOLD iconography; 7 older + younger player with Lil Blunt between; 8 warm post-Bong-Party celebration.
Tool: Google Flow, Nano Banana 2.1 (Pro for hero pieces, 2 Lite for thumbs); 2-3 variations per concept; keep a generation log; file names lb_nft_NN_game_<slug>_v1.png / lb_nft_NN_community_<slug>_v1.png; folder design/nft/lil-blunt-collection/{00..03 md, outputs/game, outputs/community, references}.
Out of scope: pass floor math, burn rules, 888 supply, Bong Party percentages, canister/ICRC-7 code, Meshy 3D spin versions.
Founder additions in chat (2026-10-19): every piece must carry the neon GREEN CIRCLE (see skill gm-game-nft-green-ring); use his real Lil Blunt images as references, never invent the character; NOT cheesy ("cool as fuck"); some pieces in the game's own style, some representing the community and stoner communities; finished set goes in a Google Drive folder.
