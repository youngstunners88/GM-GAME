## Episode 2 Narrative Lock - Jack Hammer (Jackson Mccree) Bounty -> Horse -> Auction

### Purpose
The first paid job Lil Blunt takes in the gold town and the ride to the auction:
1. Lil Blunt asks the **Sheriff** where he can get a job.
2. That reveals a **bounty** on **Jack Hammer** (official name **Jackson Mccree**).
3. The bounty is paid in **gold XAUt**.
4. A **jailor** (or the already-locked **accomplice** in the cell) confirms Jack Hammer will be at the **auction**.
5. Lil Blunt buys a **horse** at the stables with **half a Bitcoin** (Episode 1 crypto).
6. He rides for the auction under pressure: bandits / antagonists try to shoot him off the horse (ride-and-shoot set-piece).
It threads the jailhouse investigation into Auctions without a second open world.

### How Lil Blunt learns the job (locked order)
- **A - Ask the Sheriff for work** after the street draw / certificate cluster. Natural entry: an outsider with a gun looking for paid work, not a cutscene dump.
- **B - Bounty in gold XAUt.** Sheriff (or Sheriff + posted notice) reveals the bounty. Payout currency **gold XAUt** (no invented soft currency, no new token name). Keep the explanation short: dangerous man, early claims, bears, pressure on the gold road / vault side of the country.
- **C - Jailor word: he will be at the auction.** The jailor (preferred) or the accomplice in the cell confirms it. That is the time pressure: get to the auction before or as he does. No long interrogation; one clear line is enough.

### Horse purchase (locked)
Lil Blunt goes to the **horse stables** (the Episode 2 surface stable - not Episode 1 Level 3 scenery as the feature home) and **buys a horse with half a Bitcoin** (Episode 1 crypto). Same tender rule as the Armory and the saloon whiskey: real crypto from Episode 1, no soft currency. The stable hand can be minimal (one NPC or a simple buy interaction). After purchase the ride to the auction begins.

### Ride-and-shoot to the auction
A trail between the gold town and the auction (a short ride away; no full second city unless the founder expands scope). **Bandits / antagonists try to shoot Lil Blunt off the horse.** Ride + shoot set-piece in the same size class as the day-88 river mountain cover - not an open-world horse RPG. Success = arrive with the horse (or on foot if it is lost; the default fantasy is mounted). Failure pressure = delayed arrival, lost horse, or a damaged position when Jack Hammer is already in the room; exact fail branches stay light this pass.

### Auction destination
The Auction is a real Gold Mine protocol surface (claims / process / contested access). Jack Hammer's presence continues the early-claims / claim-jumper theme and the bears investigation. What happens *inside* is a follow-up pass. This lock only guarantees: Lil Blunt is sent there by the bounty job; Jack Hammer is expected there; arrival is via the horse trail under fire.
