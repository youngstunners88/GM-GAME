# Chamber 6 — Treasury / Sovereign Vault (Treasury & Phase-2 SWF)

**White-paper element:** Protocol Treasury / Sovereign Wealth Fund (Phase 2) —
houses the SWF, manages liquidity from Diamonds sales, and is the portal to a
separate legal entity handling real-world asset investments (PoW Bitcoin
mining, gold streaming). Proceeds supplement XAUT (auctions) and wBTC (Fort
Knox). (WP §Future/Phase 2.)

**Real constants (`goldmine_system.gd` treasury split):**
`TREASURY_NFT_PCT = 0.50` (to Gold Claim Cert holders),
`TREASURY_AUCTION_PCT = 0.20` (supplements weekly auctions),
`TREASURY_SWF_PCT = 0.20` (reinvested in the Sovereign Wealth Fund),
`TREASURY_FOUNDER_PCT = 0.10` (founder/operations). WP fund allocation also
sets **15%** of mining proceeds → Treasury/SWF.

## The 3D shooter/RPG encounter

An elegant, high-security sovereign treasury — the "endgame" chamber: marble
and brass, a live **Allocation Board** visualising the 50/20/20/10 treasury
split, and glass-walled cases showing the (Phase-2, "Coming Soon") real-world
asset positions.
1. **View / interact (real mechanic):** the player inspects protocol-owned
   positions and sees where treasury flow goes (the four real splits above).
   This is largely an **observation/RPG** chamber — the WP marks the SWF and
   RWA investments as Phase 2 / Coming Soon, so the game must present them as
   *upcoming*, not live. Must not fabricate live RWA returns.
2. **Endgame framing (proposed):** as the final chamber it can carry the
   Episode 2 climax — e.g. securing the treasury against a final raid, or a
   capstone encounter — but any *economic* content stays observational until
   Phase 2 is real in the protocol.

**Reward on exit:** narrative/eligibility payoff and the Episode 2 close;
treasury supplements to auctions/Fort Knox are shown as the mechanism, not
paid out as invented player rewards.
