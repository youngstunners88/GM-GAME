# Chamber 3 — Gold Rush Auction Hall (Weekly XAUT Auction)

**White-paper element:** Weekly 7-day GOLD Rush Auctions — deposit mined GOLD
into the pool to compete for **XAUT** (gold-backed token), pro-rata to your
share of the pool. (WP §Fund Allocation — 25% to weekly auctions.)

**Real constants (`goldmine_system.gd`):** `auction_gold_pool` is the live
per-level forfeit+deposit pool; XAUT payout is pro-rata (GOLD held vs. total
pool). Auction cadence is weekly / 7-day (WP).

## The 3D shooter/RPG encounter

A grand Wild-West auction hall: a raised auction floor, a live **Pool Meter**
showing total GOLD deposited and a **7-day countdown** (compressed to an
encounter timer).
1. **Deposit (real mechanic):** interact with the auction podium → commit
   GOLD into the pool. Your projected XAUT updates live as pro-rata share.
2. **Competition (proposed):** rival bidders (NPCs) also feed the pool,
   diluting your share; the player can disrupt rivals (non-lethal, thematic —
   e.g. pickaxe-knock their carts) or simply out-deposit them. This
   dramatizes "compete for XAUT" without inventing new economics — the payout
   math stays exactly pro-rata.
3. **Settle:** when the countdown ends, the auction settles and pays XAUT by
   final pool share.

**Reward on exit:** XAUT credited pro-rata to the shared balance. GOLD
deposited is spent into the pool as the WP specifies.

## Enemies / threats (PROPOSED — not in WP)
Rival bidders as soft "enemies" (economic, not necessarily combat). Whether
there is literal combat here or pure economic competition is an OPEN QUESTION
— this chamber may be the RPG/economic-puzzle counterpoint to the more
combat-heavy vault.
